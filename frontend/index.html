The attack

Your number is the key. They steal the key.

A fraudster convinces NTC, Ncell or Smart Cell to move your number onto a SIM they control. Nothing about your password changes — but every code your bank sends now arrives on their phone.

    Twenty-four minutes from SIM change to an emptied account — which is why the check has to happen before the code is sent, not after.

    The defence

    Four signals. One decision.

    Every login is scored 0–100 by fusing transparent rules with two machine-learning models — then thresholded into a decision a human can always overturn.

    Rule engine

    SIM-activation recency plus Haversine distance from your safe zones through a sigmoid curve. Fully inspectable.

    Behaviour

    Explainable red flags: device change, impossible travel, odd-hour logins, repeated SIM changes.

    Random Forest

    Supervised classifier over 11 telecom features, trained on 100,000 synthetic records.

    Isolation Forest

    Unsupervised model trained only on normal traffic — catches patterns no label ever described.

    ALLOW MONITOR VERIFY BLOCK

    VERIFY and BLOCK never auto-punish — they step up verification and notify a human.

    Know the moment

    Sudden “No Service” is not a glitch.

    Losing mobile signal for no reason is the one symptom a victim can actually see. If it happens unexpectedly, treat it as an emergency: call your operator, and freeze your account before anything else.
