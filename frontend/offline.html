First 10 minutes

  1. Call your operator from another phone and ask them to suspend the new SIM and restore yours.
  2. Call your bank's hotline (printed on the back of your card) and ask to freeze the account.
  3. Never read out an OTP — a code you didn't request means an attack is already in progress.
  4. From a trusted device, change your banking and email passwords.
