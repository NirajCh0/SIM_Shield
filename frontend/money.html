Cooling-off hold after a SIM change

The first large payment after a SIM change is the signature of an account being drained: the attacker has the number, resets the password, and moves the money before anyone notices. So for a few days after any SIM change on your number, a payment is not refused and not allowed — it is held, and releasing it needs a code sent to your email, a channel someone holding your phone number does not control.

This is the control banks already apply after an operator reports a swap. SIMShield demonstrates it end to end rather than describing it.

Try it before and after the SIM-swap trigger on your dashboard to see the same amount treated differently.
