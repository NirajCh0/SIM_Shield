Where you signed in

area only — no coordinates stored

SIMShield records a coarse area and distance band, never your exact position — enough for you to spot a sign-in that wasn't you, too little to track your movements. Turn it off with store_login_area: false in compliance.yaml.

Trusted contact

On a critical alert this person is emailed too. If an attacker holds both your phone number and your inbox, this is the channel that still gets through.

Sign-in that survives a SIM swap

Codes sent by SMS arrive at whoever holds your number. If your SIM is swapped, that is the attacker. The two options below never travel over the mobile network, so they still work when your number does not.

Passkeys

A key held by this device or a security key. It cannot be phished and cannot be texted to anyone.

Recovery codes

Ten single-use codes to print and keep somewhere that is not your phone. Generating a new set cancels the old one.

Save these now — they are shown once and cannot be retrieved again.
